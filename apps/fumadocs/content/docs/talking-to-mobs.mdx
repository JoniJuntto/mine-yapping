## Push-to-talk

Hold **V**, speak, release. That is the whole interaction.

- **Press** starts recording and picks a target.
- **Release** sends what you said and asks for a reply.
- The key is rebindable in **Options → Controls → MineYapping → Push to Talk**.

Hold it for at least a moment — if less than roughly 30 ms of audio was captured, the
mod says *Hold V a little longer so I can hear you* and sends nothing.

### Who you are talking to

1. If your crosshair is on a **living mob within 8 blocks**, that mob wins.
2. Otherwise, the **nearest living entity within 8 blocks**.
3. Never yourself, and never a dead entity.

Other players count as living entities too. If nothing is in range you get
*Look at a mob or move within 8 blocks.*

When your turn is sent, the mob turns to look at you and plays its ambient sound, so you
can tell who picked up the conversation.

### What you see

| Chat line | Colour | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `Listening to <Mob>...` | aqua | Recording started |
| `Thinking...` | gray | Turn committed, waiting on the reply |
| `You: <transcript>` | dark gray | What the transcription heard |
| `<Mob>: <reply>` | gold | The reply — also spoken aloud |
| anything else | red / yellow | An error — see [Troubleshooting](/docs/troubleshooting) |

These are local system messages. Nobody else on the server sees them.

## Typed chat

Type a message in chat while a mob is targeted or within 8 blocks, and it is treated as
speech to that mob.

<Callout type="warn" title="Your message does not reach the server">
  While a mob is in range, typed chat is intercepted by the mod and **not** sent as
  normal server chat. Move away from mobs (or into an empty area) to talk to other
  players.
</Callout>

This is how to use Mine Yapping without a microphone. Note that the interception happens
even before your key is checked: if you have not run `/login`, a message typed next to a
mob is swallowed and you get a *Run /login &lt;token&gt;* error instead of it going to
chat.

## Reactions

Right-clicking (using) or hitting a mob makes it react on its own — no key held, no
typing. The mod sends *"\<player\> just interacted with you. React naturally."* or
*"…just attacked you…"* and the mob answers in character.

This only happens when a key is saved via `/login`.

## Ambient chatter

Every **30 seconds**, the mod rolls a dice. On a hit, a random living mob within 8 blocks
starts a short conversation with you unprompted.

The chance is **0.5** by default and lives in `speechChance` in the config file. Set it
to `0` to turn ambient chatter off entirely, or `1` to make it fire on every check. See
[Configuration](/docs/configuration).

<Callout>
  Ambient chatter and reactions consume your monthly allowance like any other request.
  If you are close to the limit, lower `speechChance`.
</Callout>

## Memory

Each mob remembers the **last 4 turns** of your conversation with it, keyed by the
entity's UUID. Ask a cow "what did I just say?" and it knows; ask the cow next to it and
it has no idea.

Memory lives in the server's process, so it is cleared when the backend restarts, and it
is capped at 1000 entities (the oldest are evicted first).

A mob's assigned **personality and voice** are sticky in a stronger way — those are
stored in the database and survive restarts, so the same cow keeps sounding like the
same cow.

## Audio

Replies are spoken through your default system output device, panned left/right by where
the mob stands relative to your view, and quieter the further away it is. Playback runs
off the game thread, so the game never stutters waiting on speech.

The mod captures at 24 kHz, 16-bit mono and plays back at 24 kHz stereo. If your device
cannot do that, you get *24 kHz microphone not supported* or
*24 kHz stereo playback is not supported*.
