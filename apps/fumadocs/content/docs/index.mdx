Mine Yapping is a **client-only Fabric mod** for Minecraft: Java Edition. Hold **V**,
speak at a mob, and it answers in character — the reply appears in your chat and is
spoken through your speakers.

## How it works

The mod streams your microphone to the Mine Yapping backend, which runs
speech-to-text → LLM → text-to-speech and streams the reply audio back.

```
[Fabric mod]  hold V → 20 ms PCM frames ───────────┐
                                                   │ WebSocket
[Backend]     /api/converse/stream ────────────────┘
                 ├─ realtime transcription  → transcript
                 ├─ LLM (streamed sentences)
                 └─ ElevenLabs speech       → PCM chunks
                                                   │
[Fabric mod]  chat lines + positional audio ◄──────┘
```

The mod **never talks to the Minecraft server**. It works in singleplayer, on vanilla
multiplayer servers, and on modded servers — with only you having it installed. Nobody
else sees your conversations or hears the replies.

## What you get

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Push-to-talk** | Hold **V** (rebindable), speak, release. |
| **Smart targeting** | Whatever you look at wins; otherwise the nearest living mob within 8 blocks. |
| **Typed chat** | Type near a mob and it replies instead of the message going to server chat. |
| **Reactions** | Right-click or hit a mob and it reacts on its own. |
| **Ambient chatter** | Mobs start conversations with you on their own (tunable, can be turned off). |
| **Memory** | Each mob remembers the last 4 turns of your conversation with it. |
| **Personalities** | Write your own system prompts per mob type from the dashboard. |
| **Positional audio** | Replies are panned and attenuated by where the mob is. |

## Requirements at a glance

- Minecraft: **Java Edition 1.21.11** (Bedrock is not supported)
- **Fabric Loader 0.19.3+** and **Fabric API**
- Windows, macOS, or Linux desktop — **Android / PojavLauncher is not supported**
- A working microphone and audio output
- A free account at [mine-yapper.com](https://mine-yapper.com)

## Cost

The mod is free and open source. The hosted service gives every account **100 requests
per month** (150 if you link Twitch). You can also
[bring your own OpenAI and ElevenLabs keys](/docs/account#bring-your-own-keys) for
unlimited use, or [self-host the backend](/docs/self-hosting). If you want more than the
free allowance, you can buy [AI credits](/docs/account#ai-credits) — they unlock no
features, they just pay for the requests themselves.
