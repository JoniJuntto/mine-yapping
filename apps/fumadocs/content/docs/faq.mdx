**Is it free?**
Yes. The mod is free and open source, and the hosted service gives every account 100
requests a month (150 with Twitch linked). Optional AI credits buy extra requests once
that allowance runs out; they unlock no features. Bringing your own provider keys, or
self-hosting, stays free and unlimited.

**Do I need to install anything on my Minecraft server?**
No. The mod is client-only and never talks to the Minecraft server. It works on vanilla
and modded servers with only you having it installed.

**Will other players hear the mobs?**
No. Transcripts and replies are local to your client. Only you see the chat lines and
only you hear the audio.

**Can I get banned for it?**
It does not modify gameplay, movement, or rendering, and sends nothing to the Minecraft
server — but some servers ban client mods by policy. Check the server rules.

**Does it work on Bedrock / on my phone / on a console?**
No. Java Edition on a desktop only. Android and PojavLauncher are unsupported because
microphone capture has no working audio mixer there.

**Forge or NeoForge?**
No — Fabric only.

**Do I need a microphone?**
No. Typed chat near a mob works exactly like speaking to it.

**Which Minecraft versions?**
Minecraft Java Edition 1.21.11.

**Is my voice recorded or stored?**
No. Audio and transcripts are streamed through and discarded. Usage records contain
counts and timings only.

**Can I use my own OpenAI / ElevenLabs account?**
Yes — save your keys under **Dashboard → Account**. Those requests do not touch the free
allowance. See [Bring your own keys](/docs/account#bring-your-own-keys).

**Can I run the whole backend myself?**
Yes, it is in the same repository. See [Self-hosting](/docs/self-hosting).

**Can I change what a mob sounds like?**
Voices are assigned randomly per mob and are sticky. You control *how it talks* with
[personalities](/docs/personalities); to re-roll a voice, delete the personality it is
using.

**What language does it speak?**
Finnish by default, English if you switch the language in the dashboard. That one setting
covers the website, the mob replies, and the mod's own messages.

**How many requests does a session use?**
One per reply — including reactions and ambient chatter. Ambient chatter rolls every 30
seconds at a 50% chance by default, so turn it down with
[`speechChance`](/docs/configuration#speechchance) if you are watching your allowance.

**Does the limit roll over?**
No. It resets at the start of each UTC calendar month.
